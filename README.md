# Tek Master Blueprint

Create a responsive 5-page website mockup for T.E.K UNIMASTER ADVISORS, an education consultancy (master recruitment agent) based in Cyprus, EU. It will be built later in WordPress (Elementor/Gutenberg), so use standard, reproducible sections.

FILES

- WEBSITE_DESIGN_BRIEF.docx: single source of truth for all text, pages and sections.

- PLAIN_LOGO_TEK.png (header), MAIN_LOGO_TEK.png (footer/hero).

- Style reference: [PASTE LINK] and https://www.uniqual.education/ (take ideas on layout, section flow, cards; don't copy text, images or logos).

CONTENT

- Use the brief's English text exactly. Don't invent facts, prices or contacts. Keep [Email]/[WhatsApp] as placeholders and mark missing images as [ADD PHOTO].

- Replace emojis with consistent line icons; keep country flags.

PAGES (same menu everywhere)

Home | For Sub-Agents | For Partner Institutions | Partner Countries | Contact

Follow the sections listed in the brief for each page. Use cards, timelines for the steps, accordions for FAQs and long lists, and country cards with flag and "Popular Fields" tags. Contact page has the form from the brief.

Footer on all pages: logo, Cyprus EU, email/WhatsApp, social icons, © 2026, links to Privacy Policy | Terms & Conditions | GDPR Policy. Header: language switcher placeholder (Google Translate). Add social share buttons.

DESIGN

- Clean, professional, trustworthy, warm, lots of whitespace.

- Colors from the logos: navy (~#0B2557) primary, gold (~#B98A2E) accent, white, dark gray text, light gray sections. Extract exact hex codes.

- Fonts: serif headings (Cinzel or Playfair Display), sans body (Open Sans or Roboto).

- Desktop and mobile views (hamburger menu).

OUTPUT

Mockup of all pages (Home fully designed), then a summary of hex colors, fonts, button styles and sections per page for WordPress.
